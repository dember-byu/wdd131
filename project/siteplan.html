Site Name

Eco-Trails Tiraque (Urban Hiking & Nature Trails Guide)

Reason for choosing it: I chose the Tiraque Province in Cochabamba, Bolivia, because I previously worked there in the municipal mayor's office. While conducting technical inspections and field work, I discovered firsthand its immense, untapped ecotourism potential (high-altitude lagoons, waterfalls, and mountain landscapes). It represents an ideal and sustainable green business opportunity.

Optional domain availability: eco-trailstiraque.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this platform is to promote responsible ecotourism in the Tiraque Province through an interactive 3-page digital space. It will provide critical weather and safety information for high altitudes, an interactive hiking route catalog with dynamic filtering, and a digital gear management checklist tailored for high-mountain trekking.

Scenarios (Target Audience)

Site visitors will frequently seek answers to the following questions, which the interface will resolve dynamically:

  • Scenario 1: "I want to go on a short weekend hike with my family in Tiraque. Which routes have low difficulty, foot accessibility, and a total distance under 5 kilometers?"
  • Scenario 2: "I know Tiraque is a very cold and high-altitude region. What specific gear and medical preparations do I need to bring to prevent altitude sickness (sorojchi)?"

Typography

  • Heading Font: Roboto

    This font will be used for all headers (h1, h2, h3). It delivers a geometric, highly legible, and modern style that facilitates hierarchical reading of tourist destinations.

  • Body Font: Open Sans

    This font will be used for paragraphs, checklist items, and informational tables. It provides optimal readability on both mobile devices in the field and desktop computers.
